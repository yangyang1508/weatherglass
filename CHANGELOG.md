# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-12

First release.

- Real weather from Open-Meteo for any town, or for your current location (rounded to about 10 km).
- Rain and drizzle with water beads on the glass. Shake the pointer to wipe them.
- Snow that settles on the top edges of your windows and falls off when a window moves, closes or hides.
- Thunderstorms with lightning, fog, hail, and leaves (or spring petals) on windy days.
- Menu bar icon with the temperature in °C or °F.
- Preview menu to try any weather now.
- Options: amount, hide when an app is full screen, pause on battery power,
  hide from screenshots and screen sharing, open at login.
- Pauses while the Mac sleeps, the screen is locked or the displays are off. Hides on clear days.
